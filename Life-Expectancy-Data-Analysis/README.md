🌍 Life Expectancy Data Analysis Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

The Life Expectancy Data Analysis Dashboard is a data analytics project designed to explore global life expectancy and the factors that influence population health. The dashboard presents key health, economic, demographic, and education indicators through interactive visualizations.

The project helps identify trends in life expectancy, compare developed and developing countries, and understand relationships between health outcomes and socioeconomic factors.

🎯 Project Objectives
Analyze average life expectancy across countries and years.
Explore adult mortality trends.
Compare developed and developing countries.
Examine the relationship between GDP, schooling, and life expectancy.
Analyze vaccination indicators, including Polio, Hepatitis B, and Diphtheria.
Present key insights through an interactive dashboard.

📈 Dashboard Features
Life Expectancy Trend: Tracks changes in life expectancy over time.
Adult Mortality Analysis: Visualizes adult mortality trends by year.
Country Comparison: Compares countries based on life expectancy.
Vaccination Analysis: Compares Polio, Hepatitis B, and Diphtheria indicators.
GDP and Life Expectancy: Examines economic indicators alongside life expectancy.
Developed vs. Developing Countries: Compares health outcomes across development categories.
Interactive Filters: Filter the dashboard by country, year, and development status.
